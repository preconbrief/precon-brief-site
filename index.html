What's inside

One page that answers “should we bid this?”

The brief reads the specification for your trade and your company profile, then lays out what matters before you commit estimating hours.

Verdict and reasons

Go, Go with conditions, or No-go signal, with the three reasons behind it, so you can see the logic and disagree with it.

Eligibility

Bonding, insurance, licensing, prequalification, prevailing wage and domestic content, checked against your company profile.

Commercial risk

Liquidated damages, retainage, payment terms and schedule constraints that cost money, ranked by severity.

Scope gaps

Items that fall between trades, such as VFDs, starters and disconnects, flagged as yours, by others, or ambiguous.

Hidden costs

Required items that rarely show on the bid form: test and balance, commissioning, seismic bracing, training and warranties.

Before bid day

Forms, alternates, missing documents, and the questions to send in before the deadline.

Supply Processing and Distribution (SPD) Renovation

James J. Peters VAMC, Bronx, NY · Project 526-10-150A · Bid date and time: not found in spec

Go with conditions

Trade fit: major. Division 23 is 197 of 233 pages.

  • Six of 14 scope gaps are ambiguous.
  • High risk: night-only steam tie-ins; no damages, retainage or bonding in PDF.
  • Bid date and eligibility cannot be confirmed.

Eligibility

Bonding, insurance, licensing, prequalification, prevailing wage and domestic content: not found in spec.

Commercial risk

  • HighSteam/condensate tie-ins only 8 pm–5 am, off-season 23 05 11, p. 44
  • MediumOutage requests 48 h; major outages 15 days 01 00 00, p. 13
  • MediumPhasing per drawings; 2 weeks' notice per area 01 00 00, p. 12
  • MediumControls warranty per FAR 52.246-21; 2 h remote response 23 09 23, p. 122
  • MediumPE rigging plan; structural reinforcement at contractor cost 23 05 11, p. 58

Your scope

Division 23, 12 sections: common HVAC, noise/seismic, TAB, insulation, commissioning, DDC, hydronic and steam piping, ducts, fans, terminals, outlets, curbs. Absent: 23 05 12, 23 25 00, 23 73 00.

Scope-gap watchlist

  • AmbiguousVFDs, starters 23 05 11, p. 47
  • AmbiguousDisconnects 23 34 00, p. 218
  • AmbiguousControls vs integrator training 23 09 23, p. 112
  • AmbiguousRoof curb heights 23 05 11, p. 53
  • AmbiguousCutting, patching 01 00 00, p. 21
  • AmbiguousPermits, fees 23 09 23, p. 109
  • YoursControl wiring; firestopping; pads; duct access doors; condensate drains

Hidden costs

  • After-hours tie-ins 23 05 11, p. 44
  • Seismic restraint, 100% horizontal 23 05 41, p. 65
  • Commissioning support 23 08 00, p. 107
  • DDC training, 16 + 8 hours 23 09 23, p. 125
  • Controls warranty, 2 h remote 23 09 23, p. 121
  • Five-year controls spares 23 09 23, p. 119

Before bid day

Forms, alternates, unit prices, allowances: not found in spec. Missing: Division 00, 01 91 00, drawings.

  • Who furnishes VFDs, starters, disconnects: Div 23 or 26?
  • Who trains the VA on DDC: controls sub or integrator?
  • Which roof curb height governs?
  • Who does cutting and patching?
  • Who pays mechanical permits and fees?

Questions

Straight answers

What does it cost?

It's free while I test it with estimators, and your first 3 briefs cost nothing. If there is pricing later, you'll hear about it before it applies.

How accurate is it?

Each finding cites the spec section and page, so you can check it in seconds. When something isn't in the spec, the brief says “Not found in spec” instead of guessing. It is a fast first read, not a substitute for your judgment.

What does it not do?

It reads specifications only. Drawings, schedules and addenda that aren't in the PDF are not reviewed, and it doesn't price anything.

What happens to my spec?

Uploads go through Tally, the form provider, and are read by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, to write your brief. I don't share your files with anyone else. Please send only documents you're free to share.

Which trades does it cover?

Mechanical/HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing and Fire protection, plus an “Other” option. The brief is written for the trade you pick.

How do I stop getting emails from you?

Reply “no” to any message and you won't hear from me again.
